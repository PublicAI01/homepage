PublicAI Foundation, a foundation company incorporated under the laws of the Cayman Islands ("PublicAI", "we", "us" or "our"), operates the website at https://publicai.io/ (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit the Site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

This Privacy Policy applies only to the Site. PublicAI products that run on their own domains, such as Trajector, are governed by their own privacy documents. All documents are listed on our [Legal](/legal) page.

## INFORMATION WE COLLECT

1. Information you provide

- Business contact form: your name, email address, company name, and the message you write. We use this information to reply to your request and to keep a record of the conversation.
- Official-account verification: the handle or email address that you enter to check whether it is an official PublicAI account. We use the input only to answer your query. We do not link it to your identity.

2. Information collected automatically

- Server logs: your IP address, the time of the request, and the requested path. We use this information to limit request rates and to protect the Site against abuse.
- Analytics data: browser type, device type, pages viewed, approximate location derived from your IP address, and similar usage data collected through Google Analytics.

3. Information collected by Google reCAPTCHA

The business contact form is protected by Google reCAPTCHA. reCAPTCHA collects hardware and software information, such as device and application data, and sends it to Google to decide whether a request comes from a human. Google's handling of this information is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms).

## HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information we collect to:

- Reply to business requests sent through the contact form
- Answer official-account verification queries
- Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site
- Understand how visitors use the Site and which content is useful
- Detect, prevent, and investigate abuse or unlawful activity
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use information collected on the Site to train AI models.

## HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

We share information with the following categories of recipients:

- Service providers: companies that host the Site, deliver our email, and provide analytics and bot-protection services. They process information only on our instructions.
- Google: analytics and reCAPTCHA data, as described above.
- Legal and safety: authorities, courts, or other parties when required by law, or when necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of PublicAI, our users, or the public.
- Business transfers: a successor entity in connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets.
- With your consent: any other recipient when you ask us to share information.

## COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

The Site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and similar identifiers to measure traffic and usage. You can learn more at Google's [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and install the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
